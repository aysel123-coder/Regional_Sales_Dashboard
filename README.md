# 📊 Regional Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Regional Sales Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project developed using **Microsoft Power BI**. It provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance across different regions, product categories, and customer orders.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual insights, helping users understand revenue distribution, sales trends, product performance, and overall business operations.

This project demonstrates my skills in **data analysis, data visualization, business intelligence, and dashboard development using Power BI**.

## 🖥️ Dashboard Preview

![Regional Sales Dashboard](dashboard.png)

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this dashboard are to:

- 📈 Analyze overall sales performance across different regions.
- 🌎 Compare revenue generated by each geographical region.
- 🛍️ Identify top-performing product categories.
- 📦 Track total orders and quantities sold.
- 💰 Understand revenue contributions by product category.
- 📊 Present business data through clear and interactive visualizations.
- 🔍 Support data-driven business decisions.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard highlights three important business metrics:

| 📌 KPI | 📊 Value | 📝 Description |
|---|---|---|
| 💰 Total Sales Revenue | $9,925.68 | Total revenue generated from sales |
| 🛒 Total Orders | 28 | Total number of recorded orders |
| 📦 Total Units Sold | 232 | Total quantity of products sold |

These KPIs provide a quick overview of overall business performance.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 🌎 1. Sales by Region

**Visualization:** Horizontal Bar Chart

This chart compares total sales revenue across four geographical regions:

- Midwest
- Northeast
- West
- Southeast

**🔍 Key Insight:**

The Midwest generates the highest sales revenue, followed closely by the Northeast. The West and Southeast contribute smaller portions of total sales.

This visualization helps identify strong-performing regions and potential opportunities for sales improvement.

### 🥧 2. Revenue Share by Category

**Visualization:** Pie Chart

This chart illustrates how different product categories contribute to overall sales revenue.

Product categories include:

- 🏋️ Fitness
- 👟 Footwear
- 👕 Apparel
- ⚽ Sports Equipment
- 🚴 Cycling
- 🏊 Aquatics
- 🎒 Accessories

**🔍 Key Insights:**

- 🥇 **Fitness** generates approximately **33.49%** of total revenue.
- 🥈 **Footwear** contributes approximately **27.25%**.
- Other categories collectively account for the remaining revenue.

Fitness and Footwear are the leading revenue-generating categories.

### 📦 3. Units Sold by Category

**Visualization:** Horizontal Bar Chart

This chart displays the total quantity of products sold within each category.

**🔍 Key Insights:**

- Fitness leads in total units sold.
- Apparel and Footwear are also significant contributors to sales volume.
- Other categories show comparatively lower sales quantities.

This analysis helps identify customer purchasing patterns and product demand.

### 📋 4. Detailed Sales Transaction Table

**Visualization:** Data Table

The dashboard includes a detailed transaction-level table containing the following fields:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| OrderID | Unique order identifier |
| Region | Geographical sales region |
| Category | Product category |
| SaleAmount | Revenue generated from each order |
| Quantity | Number of units sold |
| Year | Year of transaction |
| Quarter | Business quarter |
| Month | Month of transaction |
| Day | Day of transaction |

**🔍 Purpose:**

This table allows users to review individual sales transactions and better understand the data behind the dashboard's summary metrics.
